# Design direction

Retain the original archive's restraint and emphasis on Honduras rather than introducing portfolio or sales features. Preserve the three navigation items and the nine places in their existing order.

Palette: white `#ffffff`, ink `#15252d`, muted text `#59676e`, ocean `#156274`, divider `#dde5e8`, image loading surface `#f1f5f6`. Keep colors secondary to the photography. PT Sans, locally hosted, retains the original site's typeface; regular display text and bold wordmark.

Layout:

```
Made in Honduras                         Places  Why  Who

              Honduras, an unspoiled paradise.
                Personal archive introduction

[original cover]        [original cover]        [original cover]
Place and count         Place and count         Place and count
Short description       Short description       Short description
```

The original three-column index remains legible and familiar, with two columns on tablets and one on phones. Album pages pair the place name with a short description, preserve longer original text behind “About this place”, then show uncropped photographs in justified rows. Small screens show one photograph per row. A dark viewer supports zoom, touch gestures, explicit buttons, keyboard and history. No autoplay or ornamental animation.

Plan review: a full-bleed hero, location badges, maps and new editorial copy would distract from this small archive. They are omitted. Original covers, titles, prose and image order give the page its identity. The visible image count helps visitors understand an album without adding another navigation layer.
