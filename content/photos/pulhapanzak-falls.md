This waterfall is part of the river Rio Blanco, and it's considered one of the most beautiful waterfalls in Central America due to the crystal water and the 43 meters high.

I enjoyed a unique experience in this waterfall walking across the side with a local guide until a cave below the waterfall, incredible!
