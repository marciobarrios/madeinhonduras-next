La Ceiba is a port city on the northern coast of Honduras, opened to the Caribbean Sea, and very well connected with Bay Islands and other important cities of Honduras.

The city has been officially proclaimed the "Eco-Tourism Capital of Honduras" because it has lots of great nature spots nearby, like Cayos Cochinos, Bay Islands, Pico Bonito, Cuero y Salado, Cacao Lagoon, Corozal, Cangrejal river... it's a great spot to discover and enjoy nature.

The city is also considered the "Entertainment Capital of Honduras" because the lively nightlife and his famous carnival in May.
